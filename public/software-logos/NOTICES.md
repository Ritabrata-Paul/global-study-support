# Software image sources and attribution

The website uses actual sourced product marks, or a vendor mark where noted,
for identification. No affiliation, certification or endorsement is claimed.
All names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

- Python: Python Software Foundation, https://www.python.org/community/logos/ .
- React: official React repository, fixtures/dom/public/react-logo.svg.
- Java, Docker, MATLAB, ANSYS, SPSS and Stata: Devicon project assets,
  https://github.com/devicons/devicon . MIT notice included in this directory.
- AWS, Jira, R and Power BI: gilbarbara/logos collection through Iconify,
  https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos .
- AutoCAD, Revit and QuickBooks: Simple Icons collection through Iconify,
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons . Collection licence included.
- Excel: vscode-icons collection through Iconify, https://github.com/vscode-icons/vscode-icons .
- Microsoft Project: file-icons collection through Iconify, https://github.com/file-icons/icons .
- SolidWorks: Wikimedia Commons SOLIDWORKS_Logo.svg,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SOLIDWORKS_Logo.svg .
- ETABS: official CSI product wordmark,
  https://www.csiamerica.com/site/branding/ETABS_web_Logo_150pxh_white_210202.png .
- STAAD.Pro: Bentley vendor mark, sourced from https://www.bentley.com/favicon.svg .
  This is a vendor identifier, not a claim that it is the STAAD application icon.

Raster PNG versions are generated locally from the sourced vectors using Sharp
for reliable display. Original vector sources and applicable notices are kept.
